# Sending files
To send a file, first open the Send tab. Once in, you're presented with four options, here presented from left to right:
- **Add files**: lets you add multiple files chosen from your directories
- **Add folder**: adds all the files contained in that folder
- **Send text message**: lets you type directly in the app a text message, and then send it
- **Paste**: lets you paste whatever it is in your clipboard. *Note that if it's empty, it adds to the list of files to send an empty "text.txt" item

Once all the files you want are added, you can view them in the "Files to send" section. Here you can delete it from the list, view its properties, or open an item by double-clicking on it. The button "Clean files" will clear the list by all the files added.

## Sending to devices
Since version 1.1, you can now send files to multiple devices together. Just hit the checkbox near any device that you want to send it to, and then hit the "Send files" button.

If your device doesn't show up in the Nearby devices list, hit the "Search again" button to send again a message to other devices connected to the same network in broadcast, or manually enter the IP address or hashtag of the other devices (both formats must be formatted correctly in order to be recognised).

Status (and errors) in transferring files will be shown on the bottom left part; you can also stop a transfer that started by hitting the "Cancel" button. Notifications about the transfer will also appear as a tray notification (on LocalSend32, ARM and RT).

## PIN
If you've set up to require a PIN for transfers on the other device, a window will be shown to let you enter the PIN of the other device. Once entered correctly, the transfer will proceed.

## Tray menu
The tray menu is a quick and easy way to send files without opening the whole interface. Right click on the tray icon to open the menu:
- in the *Drop files here* area, drop all the files that you want to send. All the files that are added via this interface, or the full one, will be shown in both areas
- head then in the *Send to device* area once you're ready to select the device to which you're wanting to send the files to. When doing so, the full interface will open to give you more information about the transfer
